FLY HUNT

Househeat AR · browser demo

  • A giant cartoon fly is loose in your house. Follow the buzz and the edge arrows.
  • Turn your whole body with the phone to aim. Tap anywhere to fire.
  • Walk with the stick on the bottom left, or take real steps with the phone (toggle 👣 Walk). WASD works on a computer.
  • The live camera picture stays still — only the fly, the arrows, and the virtual room move with you. iPhone Safari can't track your position in space.
  • ADS raises the blaster to your eye: iron sights, a red dot, or the Long Shot's scope (🔭 zoom, 🫁 hold breath).
  • Tap the ammo counter to reload. The fly dodges if you hold your aim on it too long.
  • Turn the silent switch off so you can hear the buzz. 🎧 Headphones work best.
