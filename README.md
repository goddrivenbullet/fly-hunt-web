# Fly Hunt (browser demo)

Play it: https://goddrivenbullet.github.io/fly-hunt-web/

A pseudo-AR (3DOF) browser demo for the Househeat game. On an iPhone, the rear camera is the background and you turn the phone to hunt a buzzing cartoon fly with toy blasters. On desktop, you play with mouse look in a virtual room.

No build step. It's plain HTML, CSS, and JS, with three.js loaded from jsDelivr.
